Our mission & vision

We're students. Some of us left Myanmar. Some of us never got the chance to know it freely at all.

WBS started on 31 January 2021, founded by a small group of us who refused to just watch from a distance. Daw Aung San Suu Kyi has been the reason we started — her belief that freedom from fear is something you fight for together, not something handed down.

We're not a big institution. We're students and young professionals scattered all over the world, connected to each other and to the Burmese diaspora wherever it lands. What we're building is simple: a way for our generation to actually do something — to learn, to speak up, to reach the people who can help Myanmar move toward democracy.

We believe in three things: integrity, knowledge, and empowerment. Not as slogans — as the actual reason a handful of us decided this was worth our time. Real change doesn't come from one person. It comes from a lot of people, all pointed the same direction.

What we focus on

Academy

We're building the next generation of leaders for Burma — training fellows to lead with the tools and confidence real change requires.

Governance & Diplomacy

Change happens on two fronts: the ground, and the rooms where decisions get made. We work both — building real relationships with Western governments and institutions to help steer Myanmar toward democracy.

Law & Justice

Daw Aung San Suu Kyi's belief in the rule of law is central to how we think. We respect the law, and we work to help our community understand why lawfulness — and just laws — matter for Myanmar's future.

Public Relations

A country's strength comes from unity. We work to build real connections — across Myanmar's ethnic groups, and with the wider world — because those relationships are what a better future is built on.

What We Do

Lobbying & Advocacy

Engaging with the European Union, the United Nations, and international NGOs to keep Myanmar on the global agenda.

Events & Campaigns

Organizing workshops, awareness events, and peaceful demonstrations to raise visibility and support for democracy.

Education & Empowerment

Supporting Burmese youth through mentorships, English language classes, and skills-based workshops.

Networking & Collaboration

Bringing students together with policymakers, diplomats, academics, and activists to create impact.
