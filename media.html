About WBS in brief

About WBS

100 words

Worldwide Burmese Students (WBS) is a student-led, Dutch-registered non-profit with members around the world. Founded on 31 January 2021, the day before the military seized power in Myanmar, it works against dictatorship and for sustainable democracy through education. The WBS Academy teaches young people about democracy, human rights, diplomacy and advocacy. WBS also lobbies the European Union, the United Nations and Western governments for action on Myanmar, and organises events, campaigns and peaceful demonstrations. It is registered as an ANBI in the Netherlands. Its founder and president is Aye Kari Soe.
