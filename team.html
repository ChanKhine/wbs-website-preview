Our Team

Powered by young people who refuse to look away.

WBS runs on passionate volunteers who believe in the power of youth and the importance of justice: students, young professionals and community members united by one goal, a free and democratic Myanmar.

Meet our Founder & President

Aye Kari Soe

It is an immense privilege and a true honour for me to represent a new wave of passionate individuals from Burma who dedicate their lives to the pursuit of a brighter future. For generations, students in Burma have valiantly fought for freedom and peace, investing their souls in the cause. Now, it is the responsibility of Generation Z to carry on this noble struggle. Despite the regime's attempts to keep us uneducated and fearful, our beloved grandmother Suu has imparted a transformative lesson, a chapter that grants us freedom from fear.

We, as the Worldwide Burmese Students, are the generation destined to continue the fight for freedom and democracy. Until law and justice prevail within our nation.

About our founder

Born in a refugee camp. Lobbying for Myanmar in Europe.

Aye Kari Soe was born in a refugee camp on the Thai–Myanmar border. Her parents had left Myanmar, and she grew up in the Netherlands, where she studied international business. She learned Burmese by watching Myanmar dramas, and she founded WBS to give young people from Myanmar the kind of training that young Europeans take for granted.

“When I work on Myanmar, it’s not politics any more. It’s my family, my home.”

Aye Kari Soe, Dawei Watch, 22 December 2022 (3:31, translated from Burmese)
