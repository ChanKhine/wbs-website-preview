Take action

Democracy is built by people who show up.

Here is what we ask of governments, companies and universities, and what you can do yourself.

What we ask

These are the changes we want to see. Each comes from our founder’s own words.

Stop investing in companies owned by the military.

Dutch pension funds and the Dutch government should stop investing in companies owned by the military.

“In the Netherlands we still have pension funds that are still investing in the companies of the military… Even though it’s legally allowed, don’t we have values as a democratic country?”

Aye Kari Soe, Foundation Max van der Stoel, 11 May 2021 (54:58)

Share practical knowledge with young people inside Myanmar.

We ask European universities to share practical knowledge with young people inside Myanmar. The founder described this work at the Foundation Max van der Stoel panel on 11 May 2021, naming Wageningen University as an example.
