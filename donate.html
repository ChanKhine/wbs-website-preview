Help build a democratic future for Myanmar.

Fund the education that makes democracy last.

Democracy lasts when people are educated, organised and ready to defend it. Your donation, whatever its size, helps us educate the next generation of Burmese leaders to build that democracy and keep it.

Your support helps fund the Academy’s courses and workshops, and the awareness campaigns and media work that keep Myanmar on the agenda.

Every year we host a benefit gala for the Worldwide Burmese Students, gathering our family and friends to celebrate the mission and the support we receive. Your contributions play a crucial role in enabling our non-profit work.

WBS is a registered Dutch non-profit (ANBI), and we publish our policy plan and annual financial statements. For a long-term partnership, please email us and we’ll get in touch as soon as possible. Thank you for being part of our journey.
